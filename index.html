MEP SUPPORT

MEP Support & Pipe Fixing

Engineered fixing for real MEP installations: heavy pipework secured with split clamps, threaded suspension rods and modular channel support assemblies.

  • Threaded rods, slotted channels, brackets and trapeze frames
